What we need · scope & delivery

Each package covers one project or apartment type, in one language. The €300 page assembles supplied, approved materials in the layout shown, with one factual correction pass. The €900 image package improves ten supplied views, with one consolidated correction round; crops do not count as extra images. The €1400 kit includes that same ten-image package, three additional detail or lifestyle frames, a branded page, a PDF and two campaign layouts. One consolidated correction round covers the kit. Other image quantities are quoted before production. Unsupported new viewpoints, 3D modelling and interactive tours are scoped separately.
