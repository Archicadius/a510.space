Bodrum Villas

The sale is made outside

In Bodrum a villa is not sold in the living room. It is sold outside: at the gate, on the terrace, in a chair facing the sea

We design the house and its garden as one place, for the way it will be shown and the way it will be lived in

The arrival

The first impression is made at the gate

Before the door opens the buyer has already seen cypress, clipped green, warm stone and a lit window behind a curtain

The living room

One room decides the house

Walnut, stone with a red vein, bronze and travertine. The sea stays open

A house at this price has to impress at first sight and live up to its status

So we work with exquisite materials: stone with a red vein, smoked walnut, cast bronze. Every wall is built like a piece of furniture, and every detail is made to be noticed

The signature style, piece by piece

Four materials carry the room

Five villas, five characters

One plan, five interiors

Five villa types mean five show homes. We will design five different styles, one for each, so every buyer finds a house of their own. The villa is then tailored to its owner, like a made-to-measure suit

The suite

The quiet end of the house

The same materials, turned down: stone behind the bed, walnut, a terrace of its own

The house continues in the garden

Half the year the terrace doors are open. So we see the plot as a set of rooms, and the buyer walks through them one by one
